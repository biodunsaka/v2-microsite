The program

A term supply contract with the structure spelled out before you sign.

Format, duration, delivery, components and support, listed below so there is nothing to interpret.

Offer structure

ElementWhat you receive
FormatA bilateral term supply agreement under standard international crude or LNG master terms, with annual volume commitment and monthly nominations.
DurationOne, three or five years. Each term has a defined start, review and renewal date.
ProductsGuyana medium sweet crude, Permian light sweet crude, and LNG from U.S. Gulf Coast liquefaction. Grades and specifications are fixed in the contract schedule.
DeliveryFOB at the load port, or CIF to your named discharge port. Laycans are confirmed 30 days ahead; cargo size matched to your receiving capacity.
Emissions dataA per-cargo methane-intensity certificate based on continuous measurement at the producing asset, delivered with shipping documents.
SupportA named supply manager, a quarterly performance review, and 24-hour operational scheduling desk coverage.

How the program works

  1. Request a proposal. Share product, annual volume, discharge port and preferred term.
  2. Receive a draft within two business days. It shows the price formula, the differential, cargo schedule and emissions reporting format.
  3. Choose your flexibility options. Add a price collar, adjust the volume tolerance band, or select early-exit terms.
  4. Sign and schedule. First cargo nominated within 60 days of signing for crude, 90 days for LNG.
  5. Review every quarter. Your supply manager reports delivered volumes, pricing against the formula and emissions data in one dashboard.

What this means for your operation

Fewer renegotiations

A fixed differential for the full term removes the quarterly repricing cycle.

Planning certainty

Committed volumes let you schedule turnarounds and storage with confidence.

Audit-ready emissions figures

Measured per-cargo data drops straight into your Scope 3 reporting.
