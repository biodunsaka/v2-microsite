What you get

Each benefit below rests on a specific feature of the program, and each feature produces a result you can measure in your own operation.

Price stability

One transparent formula

Every cargo is priced at Brent plus a fixed differential set at signing. You can budget a full term without renegotiating, and an optional collar caps your exposure in both directions.

Reliability

Supply from record-output basins

Volumes are allocated from Guyana and Permian production, which set annual records in 2025. Your term is matched to committed capacity, not spot availability.

Emissions reporting

Certified intensity data with every cargo

Each delivery carries a measured methane-intensity certificate, so your Scope 3 reporting rests on measured data rather than industry averages.

Who this program is built for

It is for

Refiners that need a steady crude slate for 12 months or more. Utilities and industrial gas users planning fuel budgets against regulated tariffs. LNG importers that must show measured emissions data to lenders and regulators.

It is not for

Spot or single-cargo buyers. Traders seeking short-dated optionality. Buyers who cannot commit to a minimum annual volume. Those needs are better served by the open market.
