Our commitments to you

Delivery guarantee

If a nominated cargo misses its confirmed laycan for reasons within our control, the differential on that cargo is waived.

Data guarantee

Every cargo ships with a measured emissions certificate. If one is missing, the cargo is reported at the asset's verified annual figure at no cost to you.

Clear exit

Exit terms are printed in the contract for every tier. We do not rely on auto-renewal.
